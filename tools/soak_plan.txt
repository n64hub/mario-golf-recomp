# Mario Golf's soak plan: one round through the game's modes and scenes, three minutes with the boot, for the ops
# repo's tools/frame_dump.mjs --soak (through tools/remote_frame_dump.sh), whose header says each step and what fails a
# soak: a Crash: line, the program exiting, or the picture standing still for 10 s. The steps are
# tools/capture_window.ps1's, plus `random`. Run it with --soak 180.
#
# A round: the logos, the intro and the title, the main menu, character select and Play Modes; a tee shot of
# Tournament on Toad Highlands (the hole's flyover, the flight, the cut to the green), Save and Quit by the save menu;
# Ring Shot's hole and a shot through its rings, Return to Menu; Training's hole picker with its hole changed at random
# for a few seconds (where v0.1.6 hung), then that hole's flyover and tee, Return to Menu; Mini-Golf's first hole and a
# putt, Return to Menu; random presses of the main menu's cursor until the time is up. Each hole's flyover is skipped
# with A, or the round doesn't fit. Each `shot` is a picture of where the round got to.
#
# Times are seconds since the program started; each until waits on the game's RAM (the USA ROM's addresses, syms/)
# and moves the later steps on by its wait, so the round keeps step with the game, not the clock. Overlays: the loaded
# byte at the head of each 0x28-byte entry of the overlay table at 0x800B5F58 (CLAUDE.md, The code): 8 the title, 3 the
# main menu and the menus after it, 4 a hole's flyover. The menu screen is 0x801EFFA0 (0 the main menu, 1 Character
# Select, 5 Play Modes, 6 a mode's course or hole select), the Play Modes cursor the low byte of 0x801323A8 (0
# Tournament, 1 Get Character, 2 Ring Shot, 3 Speed Golf, 4 Stroke, 5 Mini-Golf, 6 Training; it stays where it was
# left and wraps), the pause 1 in the low byte of 0x800C306C, and the pause menu's cursor the low byte of 0x80224924,
# whose last item, W from the first, is Save and Quit (by the save menu) or Return to Menu; the main menu is waited for
# with A pressed, which a save menu takes and the main menu's zoom drops. Keys are the keyboard's defaults: Enter
# Start, Space A, WASD the stick.
