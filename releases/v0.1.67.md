In a wide window, the red pin flag in the sky stays one flag past the 4:3 picture's left side. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.67-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.67-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The red pin flag at the window's left side:** in a wide window, as the view turned the flag in the sky out past the 4:3 picture's left side, two to four flags were drawn side by side there, a row that shrank back into one as the flag came back in. The flag's picture holds its four frames side by side, and where the game cuts the flag at the screen's side, the recompilation drew it back on from the first frame rather than its own. It is now one flag, its own frame, all the way to the window's edge ([#190](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/190)).
