Ring Shot's stage select now closes its slats over the next course, and the sun's light slides smoothly with the course. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.44-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.44-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The sun moves smoothly with the course:** at a display rate above the game's 30, as the camera panned past the sun its light and glow went sideways in uneven steps against the trees and hills, as the game puts the sun on whole pixels of its own small picture. The sun's light is now drawn where the sun is, between whole pixels, so it slides with the course ([#122](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/122)).
- **Ring Shot's stage select:** changing stage now closes the wooden slats over the board, keeps them shut while the next course loads and opens them on it, as on a console, where only two corner pieces flashed for a frame. The picture's top and bottom lines there now show the course, where a black band and a band of streaks showed ([#124](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/124)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
