The red pin flag in the sky keeps up with a fast turn of the view, and in a wide window goes on to its edges. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.63-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.63-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The pin flag in the sky in a wide window:** the red flag that floats over the pin while you aim went out of sight at the side of the old 4:3 picture, with a quarter of a 16:9 window still to go, as you turned the view to bring it toward the edge. It now goes on to the window's edge, as the course does ([#181](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/181)).
- **The pin flag in the sky as the view turns fast:** at a display rate above 30, the flag still moved in jerks once the view turned fast with the D-pad, standing still for a few frames and then jumping while the course turned smoothly behind it. It now moves with the course in every frame ([#181](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/181)).
