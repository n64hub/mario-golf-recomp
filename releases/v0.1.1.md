Widescreen for the HUD. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.1-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.1-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The HUD at the wide picture's sides:** in a window wider than 4:3 the hole's map and par sit at the top left, the wind and the golfer's face at the top right, the shot type and club at the bottom left and the lie at the bottom right, with the swing meter along the bottom in the middle. The pause menu and the menus stay in the middle 4:3. The sides are those of the Graphics tab's HUD Placement (16:9 by default), and Aspect Ratio Original plays as the console does.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
