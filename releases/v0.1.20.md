The pin's flag no longer shakes at high refresh rates, and a closed iris wipe is black to a wide window's edges. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.20-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.20-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The pin's flag and pole on a fast display:** at a refresh rate above the game's own 30 frames a second, most visibly at 120 Hz and up, the flagstick on the green now moves smoothly with the course. Before, it shook at its foot as the green slid under it between the game's own frames (issue 53). The browser page has the same fix.
- **The closed iris wipe in a wide window:** with Aspect Ratio Expand in a window wider than 4:3, the black screen between the iris closing and the next hole opening no longer shows a thin line of the course down each side.
