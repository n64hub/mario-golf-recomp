- **The ball's sprites drawn whole again:** since v0.1.34 the ball drawn as a sprite (near the camera, on the green and in the intro), its shadow and its coloured marker lost their top row whenever they were put between two of the game's pixels down the screen, so the ball looked shaved off at the top and flicked between cut and whole as it moved, as Baby Mario's ball did in the intro. They are now put on whole pixels down, where the renderer draws them whole, and still slide smoothly across. And the intro's ball, which the game draws in two halves, no longer comes apart at Mario's feet, its top half a pixel off its bottom half with grass between them: the two halves now move together ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).
