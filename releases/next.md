- **The ball's sprites drawn whole again:** since v0.1.34 the ball drawn as a sprite (near the camera, on the green and in the intro), its shadow and its coloured marker lost their top row whenever the ball's top fell between two of the game's pixels, so the ball looked shaved off at the top and flicked between cut and whole as it moved, as Baby Mario's ball did in the intro. The renderer starts such a sprite a row further into its picture; the row is now given back. And the intro's ball, which the game draws in two halves, no longer comes apart at Mario's feet, its top half a pixel off its bottom half with grass between them: the two halves now move together ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).
