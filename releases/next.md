- **The far ball moves smoothly in flight:** at a display rate above the game's 30, a ball far from the camera, drawn as a dot of colour two pixels by one, now moves on every frame the display shows, to a quarter pixel, where it held its place for a whole game frame and then stepped a pixel ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).
- **The ball's sprites drawn whole again:** since v0.1.34 the ball drawn as a sprite (near the camera, on the green and in the intro), its shadow and its coloured marker lost their top row whenever the ball's top fell between two of the game's pixels, so the ball looked shaved off at the top and flicked between cut and whole as it moved, as Baby Mario's ball did in the intro. The renderer starts such a sprite a row further into its picture; the row is now given back ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).
