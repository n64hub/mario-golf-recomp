- **The Game Boy golfers:** a new option, Game Boy Golfers on the General tab, puts Kid, Sherry, Azalea and Joe, the golfers of Mario Golf for the Game Boy Color, at character select, with the stats the game itself holds for them (issue 82). Those look like the developers' test values: Kid drives 500 yards, Sherry 400, Azalea 300 and Joe 200, far past the other golfers. On a console the four come only through a Transfer Pak holding the Game Boy game, with their stats from its save. It is Original (off) unless you turn it on.
