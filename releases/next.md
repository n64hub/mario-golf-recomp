- **The pin flag in the sky as the view turns:** at a display rate above 30, the red flag that floats over the pin while you aim moved in jerks as you turned the view with the D-pad, its pace changing 30 times a second while the course turned smoothly behind it. The game puts it on whole console pixels; it is now placed to a fraction of a pixel, so it turns with the course, and so does the label over the shot's landing point ([#166](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/166)).
