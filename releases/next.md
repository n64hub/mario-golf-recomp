- **No seam at the top of the hills:** as the camera swung over a course at the start of a hole (Mini Golf's first hole, behind the tee), a thin line of sky could flicker across the top of the picture, with the hills above it offset sideways from the ones below (issue 81). The game draws its distant hills as a stack of thin strips and cuts the one that crosses the top of the screen. In the frames drawn between the game's own, the renderer drew that strip where the game's last frame put it, while the strips below it moved on smoothly. It now moves with them. The browser page has the same fix.
