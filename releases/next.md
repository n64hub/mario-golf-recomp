- **The far hills at their own width:** in a window wider than 4:3, the hills behind the course were stretched across it, wider than the course in front of them, and whenever the sun's light was in the picture they drew only inside the 4:3 frame, with plain sky at the sides, so as the camera turned past the sun they jumped between the two widths (Training's views of each hole, a hole's flyover). They now keep the width they have in the 4:3 frame and go on to the window's edges in every frame, the sun's light behind them as the game draws it ([#139](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/139)).
- **The end credits' cuts:** at a display rate above the game's 30, a frame drawn between two of the game's own as the credits cut from one golfer to the next showed the next golfer partway from one camera to the other. The cut is now drawn as a cut.
- **A ball's flight:** a frame drawn between two of the game's own could stretch the ball's tracer into a large light-grey triangle across the course. The tracer is now drawn between frames only when all of it moved together, and otherwise where the game puts it.
