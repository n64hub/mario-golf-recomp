The Play Modes wheel turns smoothly, and the opening's fades, the end credits' cuts and a ball's flight no longer show a frame out of place. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.48-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.48-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The Play Modes wheel turns smoothly:** at a display rate above the game's 30, the modes' pictures on the Play Modes wheel moved in steps as they grew and shrank on its turn, off the wheel's pace. They now move, grow and shrink on every frame the display shows.
- **The opening's fades:** as the opening (and the title's replay of it) faded from one shot to the next, one frame showed the old shot's top half slid down over the new one. The old shot now stays where it is as it fades.
- **The end credits' cuts:** as the end credits cut from one golfer's shot to the next, one frame showed the next golfer partway between the two shots. The credits now cut straight to the next shot.
- **A ball's flight:** as a ball flew, a long light-grey triangle could flash across the course for a frame. It no longer does.
