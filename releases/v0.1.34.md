Fixes for the ball shivering over the grass at a high refresh rate and the Save Menu's text as it slides off. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.34-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.34-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The ball sits on the grass at a high refresh rate:** with the frame rate above 30, the ball near the camera, its shadow and its coloured marker move with the course between the game's frames, as in the intro's shot of Mario walking past the ball, rather than shivering over the grass. The game puts them on whole pixels of its own small picture; now they go where the ball is, to a quarter of one. A ball far away in flight still steps a pixel at a time ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).
- **The Save Menu's text stays whole as it slides off:** at a refresh rate above 30, after saving from Save and Quit, each slot's line slides off in one piece. In the frames drawn between the game's own, letters were swapped about, "There is no saved data" shown as "Ther eis nos aved data".

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
