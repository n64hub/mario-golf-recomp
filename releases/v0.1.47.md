The far hills behind the course keep their own width in a wide window and reach its edges in every frame. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.47-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.47-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The far hills at their own width:** in a window wider than 4:3, the hills behind the course were stretched across it, wider than the course in front of them, and whenever the sun's light was in the picture they drew only inside the 4:3 frame, with plain sky at the sides, so as the camera turned past the sun they jumped between the two widths (Training's views of each hole, a hole's flyover). They now keep the width they have in the 4:3 frame and go on to the window's edges in every frame, the sun's light behind them as the game draws it ([#139](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/139)).
