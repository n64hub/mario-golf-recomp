The ball's sprites are drawn whole again, and the opening's sky reaches the top of the window. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.42-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.42-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The opening's sky reaches the top:** the top few lines of the opening (and of the title's replay of it) no longer show grass and trees left over from an earlier picture: the sky goes on to the window's top edge.
- **The ball's sprites drawn whole again:** since v0.1.34 the ball drawn as a sprite (near the camera, on the green and in the intro), its shadow and its coloured marker lost their top row whenever they were put between two of the game's pixels down the screen, so the ball looked shaved off at the top and flicked between cut and whole as it moved, as Baby Mario's ball did in the intro. They are now put on whole pixels down, where the renderer draws them whole, and still slide smoothly across. And the intro's ball, which the game draws in two halves, no longer comes apart at Mario's feet, its top half a pixel off its bottom half with grass between them: the two halves now move together ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/96)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
