The banner a shot comes to rest under keeps its text on it and spins in smoothly. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.42-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.42-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The shot's banner keeps its text:** when a shot comes to rest, the banner that spins in with the lie and the distance (ROUGH, ON GREEN, 315.50Y REST) now keeps its letters on it, as on a console. In a wide window letters flew off it towards the screen's sides, and above 30 frames a second a letter now and then stood over its neighbour or under the line. It also spins in smoothly to its rest, its letters turning with it to the end: in a wide window it jumped halfway as it reached the middle, and above 30 frames a second its letters went level a moment before it did ([#117](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/117)).
