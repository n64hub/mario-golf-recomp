Wario's arm stays on his shoulder as he shakes his fist. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.26-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.26-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **Wario's arm no longer jumps as he shakes his fist:** on the Play Modes screen, at a display rate above 30 frames a second, Wario's upper arm dropped to his belly for a frame or two while his glove stayed up at his face, each time he shook his fist. The frames drawn between the game's own now move each of a golfer's joints whole, from where it was to where it goes (issue 80). The browser page has the same fix.
