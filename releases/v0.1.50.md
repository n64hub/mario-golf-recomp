The flyover before Yoshi's Island's holes 14 and 17 moves smoothly at a high refresh rate, Ring Shot's slats close over the whole window, and Character Select's text draws cleanly in a wide window. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.50-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.50-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **Yoshi's Island's flyovers at a high refresh rate:** on holes 14 and 17, the flyover before the hole went on at the game's own 30 frames a second for a second or two after the doors open on it, so on a display faster than that it moved in jerks. It now moves as smoothly as every other hole's. The doors themselves already slide out to the window's edges, as on a console ([#155](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/155)).
- **Ring Shot's slats over the whole picture:** in a window wider than 4:3, the wooden slats that close over the course as you pick another stage covered only the 4:3 frame, so the course went on showing at the left and right sides, and in a thin strip along the top and bottom, even while they were shut. They now close to the window's edges: Mario's picture stays at its 4:3 width in the middle, and plain planks run on past it to each side, each row's planks moving with it ([#153](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/153)).
- **Character Select's text in a wide window:** with Aspect Ratio Expand, the menus' text drew a thin line of the font's next letters round it at a high resolution: white lines over and under the yards beside Drive, and coloured dashes under the shot shape (Straight, Draw). It now draws only its own letters, as on a console ([#152](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/152)).
