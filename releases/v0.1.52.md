The big words a shot or a hole ends on move smoothly at a high frame rate. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.52-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.52-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The big words at a high frame rate:** at a display rate above 30, the big words a shot or a hole ends on (NICE SHOT!, NICE ON!, CHIP-IN!, HOLE-IN-ONE!, BIRDIE, PAR and the rest) lost letters, doubled them or mixed them up in the frames drawn between the game's own as they slid in and squashed, so they seemed to step at 30 frames a second. Each letter now moves from where it was to where it is going, and the word stays whole ([#154](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/154)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
