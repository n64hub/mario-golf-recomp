A fix for the top edge of the Mini-Golf flyover. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.37-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.37-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **No dark-blue blocks along the top of the Mini-Golf flyover:** at a refresh rate above 30, as the camera tips down over the course, the picture's top row no longer flashes the sky's dark blue across the distant hills, and the hills' top rows stay in line with the rows under them. In the frames drawn between the game's own, the strip of hills just cut at the top of the screen was drawn half a row low, and nothing covered the row above it ([#100](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/100)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
