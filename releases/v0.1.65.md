The pond no longer shows over the grass at the window's edges on Toad Highlands' fourth tee. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.65-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.65-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The pond at the window's edges:** on a wide window, at Toad Highlands hole 4's tee, flat strips of the pond showed along the left and bottom edges over the tee's grass while the view held still, and went as it turned. While the view is still, the game redraws the course from a picture it keeps, with the depth it keeps of it, and that depth stopped at the 4:3 frame's border, so the water drawn each frame was laid over the grass past it. The kept depth now reaches the window's edges too ([#182](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/182)).
