On a high shot, with the camera tipped up, the far hills reach the bottom of a wide window. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.70-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.70-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The far hills at the bottom edge:** in a window wider than 4:3, on a shot high enough that the camera tips up and the horizon drops to the bottom of the picture, the far hills stopped on a straight line 8 console lines above the bottom, with flat sky under them, while the trees and the ground in front ran on to the edge. The game cuts its hills off where its own border begins, and the recompilation draws the course through that border; the hills now go on through it too ([#184](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/184)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
