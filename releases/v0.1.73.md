No more stripes across the top of the picture as the camera cuts to the ball's flight. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.73-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.73-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The camera's cut to the ball's flight:** as the camera cuts from the tee to the ball in the air, a row of dashed hills or a band of vertical stripes no longer flashes across the fog at the top of the picture for a frame. It showed with the frame rate above the game's own, at a high view such as Koopa Park's second hole ([#207](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/207)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
