The sky is the pale one a console draws, with no dark-blue gaps along the top of the Mini-Golf flyover. Like v0.1.0 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64`: the 32 MiB cartridge, SHA-1 `e2c4e7a905b29529b49a1619a401fe699224829b`, or the common 24 MiB dump of it, which the launcher pads back).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.57-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioGolfRecompiled-v0.1.57-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioGolfRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2).

**What's new:**

- **The sky:** the sky's colour runs from a deep blue overhead to a pale one at the horizon, and the recompilation blended it between those across the screen differently from a console (with perspective, as a modern GPU does, where the console's chip blends a colour straight across the screen). So the deep blue came much further down: on the tee the sky was deep blue nearly to the hills, and in the Mini-Golf flyovers, as the camera looked low, jagged dark-blue gaps showed between the far hills, pipes and towers along the top of the picture, where a console shows pale sky. The recompilation now blends every colour the game shades as a console does, so the sky is pale and cloudy as on a console ([#170](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues/170)).

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/mario-golf](https://n64hub.com/mario-golf/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-golf-recomp/issues).
